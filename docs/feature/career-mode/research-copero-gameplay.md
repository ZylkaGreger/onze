# Copero gameplay — second study (2026-08-01, live)

## What they do that we should beat
1. **OVR as an amber BADGE** (rounded pill, `50` / `64`) — not plain text in a column.
   Reads instantly, feels like a rating. Ours is grey text.
2. **Age chips** — every age 16..38 is a rounded chip; the current one is highlighted.
   The unplayed ones are visible and dim, so the WHOLE LIFE is on screen from move one.
3. **The row that just resolved is tinted red** with a RED age chip — a strong
   "this just happened to you" signal. We have no such highlight.
4. **Stat icons in the row** (shirt/ball/boot) instead of repeating headers.
5. **National-team row pinned at the bottom of the table**, always visible.
6. **Offers are 3-ACROSS horizontally** with crest + league badge — all options visible
   without scrolling.

## What we already have that they do NOT
- Market value in the table (they show it only in the header)
- A resolution card explaining what the decision did (they show nothing — you must
  diff the table yourself)
- Rating sparkline
- Share of available minutes
- The Road Not Taken counterfactual
- Year-by-year rows (they use 2-year blocks)
- Par comparison
- Pace presets

## Verdict
Their advantage is almost entirely VISUAL ENCODING (badges, chips, row tinting,
crests), not information. Ours is richer in content and thinner in presentation.
Adopt their encodings, keep our depth, and we are ahead on both.
