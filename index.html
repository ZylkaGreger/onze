Onze — a daily football Wordle

    
Onze is a free daily football (soccer) game — a Wordle for football fans, plus a career
       simulator. Every day there's one new puzzle and one new career, the same for everyone,
       with a spoiler-free result you can share. No account, no sign-up.

    
How to play

    
    
      
  1. Squads: you're shown five mystery club-and-season line-ups. Name any one player who was in each squad that season.
    2. 
      
  2. Type a player's name and press Enter — autocomplete helps, and spelling or accents don't have to be exact.
    3. 
      
  3. You get six guesses. A row turns green when you've named a player from that club.
    4. 
      
  4. Mystery player: guess a hidden footballer from clues that unlock one at a time — the fewer clues you need, the better your score.
    5. 
      
  5. Career: start as a 16-year-old at the day's club, pick your position, and make around
          eleven decisions — stay and fight for your place, go on loan, chase game time or chase
          prestige. Events land along the way, from a cup final on a bad ankle to a first
          international call-up. Retire with a year-by-year career table, a trophy cabinet, caps for
          your country and a score out of 100.

FAQ

    
Is there a Wordle for football?
      
Yes — Onze is a daily football Wordle: one new puzzle a day, the same for everyone, with a shareable spoiler-free result.

    
Is Onze free?
      
Completely free, and you don't need an account or sign-up.

    
Is there a football career simulator?
      
Yes — Career mode. Everyone starts the same 16-year-old at the same club each day, so careers
         are comparable, but they fork completely on your decisions. One career per day.

    
When does the puzzle change?
      
A new puzzle appears every day at midnight UTC, so players everywhere get the same daily puzzle.

    
Do I have to spell names perfectly?
      
No — matching is lenient, so accents and small spelling differences are fine, and the autocomplete will help you.
